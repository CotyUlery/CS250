# CS Program Portfolio Reflection

## How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating user stories help with this?

I interpret user needs by identifying what the user wants to accomplish and translating those needs into clear requirements for the program. It is important to consider the program from the user's perspective and focus on the functionality that will provide the most value. User stories are helpful because they describe a feature or requirement in simple terms, usually focusing on who the user is, what they need, and why they need it. This helps break larger requirements into smaller, manageable pieces of work that can be prioritized and developed during sprints. Creating user stories also helps developers stay focused on the needs of the user rather than simply adding features without understanding their purpose.

## How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?

I approach program development by breaking a larger project into smaller tasks and working through them in an organized and iterative way. Instead of trying to complete every part of a project at once, I believe it is more effective to develop, test, review, and improve the program throughout the development process. In my future work, I hope to incorporate Agile practices such as maintaining and prioritizing a product backlog, creating user stories, planning work in manageable sprints, and holding regular reviews and retrospectives. These processes can help keep development organized while also allowing changes to be made when requirements or user needs evolve.

I also want to continue using sprint planning to establish realistic goals and daily communication when working with a team. Reviewing completed work with stakeholders and reflecting on what went well and what could be improved are important practices that can lead to better products and more effective development processes.

## What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?

Being a good team member in software development means communicating clearly, being dependable, and working toward the shared goals of the project. Every team member should be willing to contribute their knowledge, listen to feedback, and respect the ideas and responsibilities of others. Good teamwork also requires accountability, meaning that team members should take responsibility for their assigned work and communicate early if they encounter problems or need assistance.

I believe a good software development team also creates an environment where members can ask questions, share ideas, and learn from mistakes. Agile development emphasizes collaboration and continuous improvement, so being a good team member means not only completing individual tasks but also helping the entire team succeed. By communicating openly, supporting other team members, and participating in activities such as sprint reviews and retrospectives, developers can work together to continuously improve both the product and the way the team works.
